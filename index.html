Common Challenges

Modern Desk Work
Takes a Toll

IT professionals face a unique combination of physical and mental stressors that most workplace programmes consistently miss.

🦴

Neck & Back Pain

Prolonged sitting and poor monitor positioning create chronic musculoskeletal strain that builds silently over time.

👁️

Digital Eye Strain

Hours of screen exposure causes dryness, blurred vision, headaches, and long-term visual fatigue.

🧠

Mental Fatigue & Burnout

Cognitive overload, tight deadlines, and always-on culture lead to brain fog, exhaustion, and productivity loss.

⚡

Workplace Stress

Chronic work pressure manifests physically as neck tension, shoulder stiffness, and disrupted sleep patterns.

Poor Workstation Setup

Most home and office setups are ergonomically misaligned, creating cumulative postural and musculoskeletal strain.

📉

Reduced Productivity

Physical discomfort and mental exhaustion compound, silently eroding focus, creativity, and output quality.

Beyond Physical Pain

Mental Fatigue &
Workplace Stress

Long screen hours, deadlines, and workplace pressure affect physical and mental wellbeing in ways most people never connect.

"Stress and mental fatigue contribute to neck tightness, headaches, shoulder stiffness, poor posture, eye fatigue, and reduced focus. Workplace wellness is not only about ergonomics — mental wellbeing matters equally."
— ErgoOT Clinical Perspective, based on Occupational Therapy principles
🌫️

Signs of Mental Fatigue

  • Brain fog and difficulty thinking clearly
  • Reduced focus and concentration
  • Low motivation and energy drain
  • Irritability and emotional exhaustion
  • Productivity decline over time
💆

Stress & Your Body

  • Neck tightness and shoulder stiffness
  • Tension headaches and migraines
  • Muscle tightness across the back
  • Poor posture from sustained tension
  • Sleep disturbance and fatigue cycles
✅

Healthy Work Habits

  • Structured stretch and movement breaks
  • Hydration reminders every 60 minutes
  • Mindful breathing between tasks
  • 20-20-20 screen break rule
  • Sleep hygiene and recovery rituals
🔗

The Mind-Body Connection

  • Stress amplifies physical pain sensitivity
  • Mental exhaustion worsens posture habits
  • Poor sleep degrades pain tolerance
  • Recovery from both is deeply linked
  • OT addresses the whole person
